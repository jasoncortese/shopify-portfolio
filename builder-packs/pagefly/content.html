The details, before you decide

Get the fit right.

How do I choose my phone model?

Check the exact model name in your device settings. Camera openings, buttons and dimensions can differ between phones with similar names.

Do I need all three pieces?

No. Start with the case and select only the extras you want. Your total updates before you add the kit to the sample bag.

What should I check for charging and grip compatibility?

For a real purchase, confirm your device connector, charger requirements and grip attachment with the product specifications. This design study does not claim tested charging or drop-protection performance.
