01 / START WITH THE EVERYDAY

The best accessory is the one you actually use.

The phone is already coming with you. The question is what deserves to come along. A useful setup starts with the moments you repeat: checking the map, taking a photo, answering a message, charging at your desk.

That is the idea behind the Tether concept. One muted palette. Three straightforward pieces. A case for your device, an optional grip for the way you hold it, and a cable for when you stop moving.

Less to think about.
More room for the day.

02 / FIT BEFORE FEATURES

Get the basics right first.

A case should match your exact phone model. Camera openings, button positions and overall dimensions can differ even between phones with similar names. Check your model in your device settings before choosing.

Your everyday-kit checklist
PieceWhat to check
CaseExact device model, button access and camera clearance.
GripAttachment compatibility and comfort in your hand.
CableConnector type, length and your charger’s requirements.

Look beyond the color match. A comfortable grip and the right connector matter more than an extra feature you never use.

03 / BUILD AROUND YOUR ROUTINE

A little less friction, by design.

Only need a case? Keep it simple. Prefer a grip when you’re taking photos? Add one. Want a coordinated desk setup? Include the braided cable. The right combination is the one that makes sense for you.
