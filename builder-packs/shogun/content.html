6 costume ideas

01

Celestial witch

A little velvet, a little stardust. Let one dramatic hat do the talking.

Easy layersLook 01
Make it your own
Build this look: Celestial witch
  • Start with a black dress, suit or jumpsuit.
  • Add a pointed hat and a celestial accessory.
  • Finish with shoes you can comfortably walk in.
02

After-hours vampire

Old-world drama with a modern edge. The cape makes the entrance.

Easy layersLook 02
Make it your own
Build this look: After-hours vampire
  • Pair a dark outfit with a high-collared cape.
  • Add a vintage-style brooch or a ruffled shirt.
  • Make it a duo with complementary colors.
03

Retro space traveler

Silver textures and cosmic optimism. Permission to be a little extra.

Statement lookLook 03
Make it your own
Build this look: Retro space traveler
  • Build on a silver or grey outfit.
  • Add metallic accessories and playful antennae.
  • Coordinate silver and white for a group theme.
04

Midnight disco

A glint of sequins, a favorite dance track, and absolutely no wallflower energy.

Easy layersLook 04
Make it your own
Build this look: Midnight disco
  • Choose one sequined or metallic piece.
  • Pair with flared trousers or a flowing skirt.
  • Add tinted glasses and comfortable dancing shoes.
05

The artful getaway

Black-on-black, a simple eye mask, and a very convincing alibi.

Closet-friendlyLook 05
Make it your own
Build this look: The artful getaway
  • Start with black trousers and a striped or black top.
  • Add a soft eye mask or a dark beret.
  • Use an empty canvas tote as your playful prop.
06

Twilight garden fairy

Moody florals meet delicate wings. A little less sweet, a little more spellbinding.

Statement lookLook 06
Make it your own
Build this look: Twilight garden fairy
  • Choose a plum, moss or black base outfit.
  • Layer soft floral details and lightweight wings.
  • Keep wings small enough for crowded spaces.
