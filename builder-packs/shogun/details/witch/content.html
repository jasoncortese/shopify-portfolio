Portfolio shopping guide: Etsy links are non-affiliate examples. No partnerships or commissions. AI-generated imagery illustrates styling, not the linked products.

Your three-piece outfit

  1. Start with an all-black outfit you feel good in.
  2. Add a pointed velvet hat and one celestial accessory.
  3. Finish with your own boots or comfortable flats.

Make it your own

Skip the temptation to pile on every spooky accessory you can find. A soft velvet finish, a generous brim, and a tiny touch of celestial jewelry give this look its mood. Keep the shoes comfortable and the rest of the palette simple. The hat gets the entrance; you get an outfit you can actually spend the evening in. On a shorter frame, check the hem length with your boots so the outfit moves easily with you.

The styling shortcut: repeat one metallic detail in your earrings or necklace, then stop.
