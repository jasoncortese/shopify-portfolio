Your three-piece outfit

  1. Pair dark trousers with a white or textured shirt.
  2. Add a cape and a small brooch or neck ribbon.
  3. Check the hem length with the shoes you plan to wear.

Make it your own

You do not need a head-to-toe Victorian wardrobe to make it work. A brooch, a ribbon at the neck, or a shirt with a little texture can carry the idea. Going as a pair? Agree on black and burgundy, then let each person choose their own silhouette. It feels coordinated without looking like you bought identical boxes.

The styling shortcut: choose one rich accent color and let the rest stay dark.
